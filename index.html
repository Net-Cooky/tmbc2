What Is a Digital Billboard & How Do They Work?

Up to 10 Different ads per week
Up to 4 Creative messages in rotation

A digital billboard is a high-resolution LED screen that displays multiple advertising messages in a constant rotation. Instead of being locked into a single static print, this technology provides a versatile, interactive platform for delivering messages to your exact target audience.

Throughout the course of a week, each of our digital billboards can showcase up to 10 different ads. Additionally, these advanced billboard advertising boards can feature up to 4 creative messages in rotation during a single campaign giving you total flexibility to schedule specific times or update your messaging in real time. This makes them a highly dynamic extension of traditional billboard advertising services.
